## Source-fidelity corrections made in review

The maintainer asked why reason.com was the primary source for ACI-050-K. **It
shouldn't have been.** Reason is a libertarian magazine; on this story it only
summarized the Washington Post. Making it the reference `source` broke §6A (cite the
originating outlet) and §8 (draft from the primary text). A line-by-line re-check of
**both** of today's entries against verbatim re-fetches then turned up more
snippet-sourced or inferred claims:

- **ACI-050-K:** the first redraft made WaPo the `source` and kept Reason as
  corroboration. At the maintainer's direction, Reason was then **dropped entirely**,
  and the entry was redrafted from the Post's full text, which the maintainer supplied.
  That text also showed how much the secondhand version had missed: the White House's
  denial that the departure was code-related, McCrery's own non-explanation, the Baranes
  and Clark statements, the meeting notes ("Life safety — big concern"), and the
  documentary corroboration. "Resigned" is the Post's word. The earlier "withdrew
  after" had come from Reason's paraphrase. The `reason.com` gate mapping added
  earlier this run was removed, since nothing cites it.
- **ACI-405:** Dropped as not in NBC: "footage destroyed," "1942," and "Beatrice Welles"
  (all snippet material), plus "no litigation was reported" (inferred from silence).
  Fixed attribution: "not consulted" was the estate's statement, not Reeder speaking to
  NBC. Saatchi's confirmation went to CNBC, per NBC. CNBC isn't credited in prose
  because no CNBC link was retrieved.

Every sentence and quote in both entries now traces to a full-text source: the
Washington Post (Blaskey & O'Connell, maintainer-supplied) for ACI-050-K and NBC News
(Daniel Arkin, verbatim re-fetch) for ACI-405. Lesson for the routine: a WebFetch

## Tooling change this run: `maintainer` ledger method

WaPo stayed 403-blocked, including its AMP URL, so the maintainer pasted the article
in-session. The ledger accepted only `fetch`, `search_result` or `api`, and logging
pasted text as a fetch would have been false. So `scan/log_retrieval.py` now also
accepts `--method maintainer`, for full text the maintainer supplies for a source the
tools can't reach, logged at the grade actually supplied. The provenance gate matches
on outlet and grade and needed no change. CLAUDE.md §6A and DAILY_SCAN_ROUTINE.md
Step 2A document the new method. Veto it on review if you'd rather handle supplied
text differently.
